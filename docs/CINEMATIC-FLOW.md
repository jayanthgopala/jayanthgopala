# Cinematic sequence — storyboard and Google Flow prompts

The opening of the portfolio is a scroll-scrubbed film: six generated clips
chained into one continuous shot, extracted to a frame sequence, and advanced by
scroll position rather than by a clock. Scroll forward and the camera moves
forward; stop and it holds; scroll back and it reverses.

**Status: PROMPTS CREATED. NO VIDEO GENERATED.** Nothing below exists as footage
until you generate it in Google Flow and hand the files back.

---

## The world

Deliberately *not* an arctic/ice world — that is the reference site's territory,
and the point is to hit the same quality register with our own subject.

- A vast dry plain under deep fog. Bone, ash and warm grey; no blue.
- Monolithic dark stone structures standing in it, at architectural scale.
- One figure, alone, walking. Seen from behind for the entire film — we never
  see a face, which is both stronger cinematically and removes the single
  hardest thing for a generator to keep consistent across six clips.
- Fine airborne dust, not snow.
- Overcast toplight, no visible sun, no hard shadows.
- One warm light source, and it appears only in the final chapter.

## The arc

Six chapters, mapped onto the portfolio's existing seven acts.

| # | Chapter | Portfolio act | One visual idea |
|---|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1 | The Horizon | I — Horizon | A figure alone above an enormous empty plain |
| 2 | The Walk | II — The Walk | He descends into it and begins crossing |
| 3 | The First Structure | III — Statement | One monolith resolves out of the fog |
| 4 | The Field | IV — The Work | Many more, in rows, receding |
| 5 | The Assembly | VI — The System | Fragments rise and lock into one structure |
| 6 | The Signal | VII — The Signal | Pull back; a single warm light at its centre |

Act V (The Record) stays as HTML below the film — a chronology is a reading
task, not a shot.

---

## How to generate these

1. **16:9, every clip.** Do not mix ratios.
2. **Generate in order.** After each clip, export its **final frame** and supply
   that frame as the reference image for the next chapter. This is the whole
   continuity mechanism — without it the figure's clothing, the terrain and the
   light will drift between chapters and the film will read as six unrelated
   shots.
3. **Longest duration available**, ideally 8s. Six clips ≈ 48s of film, which is
   the right budget for a full-page scroll.
4. Send me the files (MP4 preferred). Partial sets are fine — I will build what
   exists and tell you what is missing.

If a chapter comes back wrong, only that chapter is regenerated. The build is
structured so any single clip can be replaced without touching the others.

## CHAPTER 1 — THE HORIZON

**PURPOSE** Establish the scale of the world and the smallness of the figure.
The opening frame of the entire site.

**REFERENCE IMAGE** None — this clip defines the look. Everything after it
inherits from its final frame.

**START STATE** Extreme wide. A lone figure in a long dark coat stands on a
ridge, back to camera, centred, occupying maybe an eighth of the frame height.
Below and beyond: an enormous flat plain buried in fog. No structures visible.

**END STATE** Camera has moved closer and slightly lower. The figure now
occupies about a quarter of frame height, still centred, still facing away. Far
ahead in the fog, the faint vertical silhouette of a single tall structure has
just become perceptible.

**GOOGLE FLOW PROMPT**

```
Cinematic wide shot on an anamorphic lens. A solitary figure in a long dark
coat stands on a rocky ridge, seen from directly behind, facing away toward an
immense flat plain buried in deep fog. Bone and ash grey palette, warm neutral,
no blue. Heavy atmospheric haze with strong aerial perspective so distance
fades to pale grey. Overcast toplight, no visible sun, no hard shadows. Fine
airborne dust drifting slowly. The camera performs one continuous slow dolly
forward and slightly down, closing on the figure from behind at a steady
unhurried rate. The figure remains still and does not turn. Near the end of the
move, the faint vertical silhouette of a single tall monolithic structure
becomes barely perceptible far away in the fog. Photorealistic, muted, film
grain, high dynamic range, desaturated.
```

**CAMERA** One continuous slow dolly forward and slightly down. No pan, no
roll, no zoom, no cuts.

**SUBJECT MOTION** None. The figure stands still throughout.

**ENVIRONMENT** Slow fog drift and falling dust only. Terrain static.

**LIGHTING** Flat overcast, constant for the whole clip.

**CONTINUITY LOCK** Coat length and silhouette, ridge geometry, fog density,
palette. These become the reference for all five remaining chapters.

**NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS** No text. No logos. No watermark. No camera cuts. No
lens flare. No sun. No additional people. The figure must not turn around or
show a face. No blue or teal grade. No snow.

**DURATION** 8s · **ASPECT** 16:9 · **POST** None; used forward.

## CHAPTER 2 — THE WALK

**PURPOSE** Put the figure in motion. This is where the viewer understands they
are travelling, not looking at a picture.

**REFERENCE IMAGE** Final frame of Chapter 1.

**START STATE** Identical to Chapter 1's final frame — figure from behind on the
ridge, distant structure faint in the fog.

**END STATE** The figure has descended onto the plain floor and is mid-stride,
walking away from camera. The distant structure is now clearly resolved as a
tall dark monolith, though still far off.

**GOOGLE FLOW PROMPT**

```
Cinematic tracking shot. The same solitary figure in a long dark coat, seen
from directly behind, begins walking forward, descending from a rocky ridge
down onto an immense flat plain buried in deep fog. The camera follows behind
at a constant distance and constant height, moving smoothly with him, keeping
him centred and always seen from the back. Bone and ash grey palette, warm
neutral, no blue. Heavy atmospheric haze, overcast toplight, no visible sun, no
hard shadows. Fine airborne dust. The coat moves slightly with the walk. As he
advances, a tall dark monolithic stone structure ahead resolves gradually out
of the fog and becomes clearly visible, still distant. Photorealistic, muted,
film grain, desaturated.
```

**CAMERA** Constant-distance follow from behind. No orbit, no height change.

**SUBJECT MOTION** A steady, unhurried walk away from camera. No turning.

**ENVIRONMENT** Fog thins slightly ahead to reveal the monolith. Nothing else.

**LIGHTING** Unchanged from Chapter 1.

**CONTINUITY LOCK** Same figure, same coat, same palette, same fog density,
same camera height.

**NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS** No text. No logos. No cuts. No sudden zoom. The figure
must not turn or show a face. No additional people. No change in lighting.

**DURATION** 8s · **ASPECT** 16:9 · **POST** None.

## CHAPTER 3 — THE FIRST STRUCTURE

**PURPOSE** First encounter with scale. The structure should feel far larger
than expected.

**REFERENCE IMAGE** Final frame of Chapter 2.

**START STATE** Figure walking on the plain, monolith clearly visible ahead at
middle distance.

**END STATE** Camera at the base of the monolith, tilted up its face. The
structure fills most of the frame and its top is lost in fog. The figure is
small at the lower edge.

**GOOGLE FLOW PROMPT**

```
Cinematic shot. The camera continues following the walking figure from behind
across a foggy plain toward an enormous monolithic dark stone structure, then
slowly cranes upward and tilts up the face of the structure as it arrives at
its base. The structure is vast, rectilinear, weathered stone, its top
disappearing into fog. The figure becomes small at the bottom of the frame.
Bone and ash grey palette, warm neutral, no blue. Heavy atmospheric haze,
overcast toplight, no hard shadows, fine airborne dust. One single continuous
camera move, slow and controlled. Photorealistic, muted, film grain,
desaturated, immense sense of scale.
```

**CAMERA** Continuous forward move that becomes an upward crane and tilt. One
move, no cuts.

**SUBJECT MOTION** The figure keeps walking, then slows as he reaches the base.

**ENVIRONMENT** Fog static. The structure does not move in this chapter.

**LIGHTING** Unchanged.

**CONTINUITY LOCK** Same figure and coat, same palette and fog, stone material
consistent with the silhouette established in Chapter 2.

**NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS** No text or carved symbols on the structure. No logos.
No cuts. No lens flare. The structure must not move, morph or duplicate.

**DURATION** 8s · **ASPECT** 16:9 · **POST** None.

## CHAPTER 4 — THE FIELD

**PURPOSE** The reveal — there is not one structure, there are many. This is the
chapter the project work is anchored to.

**REFERENCE IMAGE** Final frame of Chapter 3.

**START STATE** At the base of the first monolith, looking up.

**END STATE** High wide aerial. Dozens of monoliths stand in loose rows across
the plain, receding into fog in every direction. The figure is a speck.

**GOOGLE FLOW PROMPT**

```
Cinematic aerial reveal. The camera lifts smoothly from the base of an enormous
monolithic stone structure, rising and moving forward past it, gradually
revealing an immense field of similar dark monolithic structures standing in
loose rows across a foggy plain, receding into haze in every direction. The
lone figure below becomes a small speck. Bone and ash grey palette, warm
neutral, no blue. Deep atmospheric fog, strong aerial perspective, overcast
toplight, no hard shadows, fine airborne dust. One single continuous rising
camera move, slow and majestic, no cuts. Photorealistic, muted, film grain,
desaturated, vast scale.
```

**CAMERA** One continuous rise-and-push. No cuts, no roll.

**SUBJECT MOTION** The figure continues walking, tiny, far below.

**ENVIRONMENT** Static structures; only fog and dust drift.

**LIGHTING** Unchanged.

**CONTINUITY LOCK** Stone material and proportions match Chapter 3's monolith.
Same palette and fog.

**NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS** No text. No logos. No cuts. No structures appearing or
disappearing mid-shot. No modern buildings, vehicles or lights.

**DURATION** 8s · **ASPECT** 16:9 · **POST** None.

## CHAPTER 5 — THE ASSEMBLY

**PURPOSE** The world does something. This is the only chapter where the
environment itself is the actor.

**REFERENCE IMAGE** Final frame of Chapter 4.

**START STATE** High wide over the field of monoliths. Sky empty.

**END STATE** An enormous structure has assembled in the air above the field
from rising fragments, now locked together and still.

**GOOGLE FLOW PROMPT**

```
Cinematic wide shot high above a foggy plain covered in dark monolithic stone
structures. Large stone fragments rise slowly and weightlessly from the field
below and drift upward, then rotate and lock together in the air to form one
enormous suspended structure above the plain. The assembly is slow, deliberate
and physically believable, like a controlled reconstruction. The camera holds a
slow forward drift throughout and does not cut. Bone and ash grey palette, warm
neutral, no blue. Deep fog, overcast toplight, no hard shadows, fine airborne
dust. Photorealistic, muted, film grain, desaturated, immense scale.
```

**CAMERA** Slow forward drift, nearly locked. The motion in this shot belongs to
the objects, not the camera.

**SUBJECT MOTION** Fragments rise, rotate, and assemble. Nothing explodes.

**ENVIRONMENT** The field below stays intact — fragments come from spare ground,
not by dismantling the monoliths.

**LIGHTING** Unchanged.

**CONTINUITY LOCK** Same stone material, palette, fog and light.

**NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS** No text. No logos. No explosions. No debris shower. No
duplication or morphing of fragments. No cuts. No magical glow — the light
belongs to Chapter 6.

**DURATION** 8s · **ASPECT** 16:9 · **POST** If assembly comes out unstable,
generate it in reverse (assembled → dispersed) and I will reverse the clip.

## CHAPTER 6 — THE SIGNAL

**PURPOSE** The close. The only warm light in the entire film, and the moment
the film hands over to the site.

**REFERENCE IMAGE** Final frame of Chapter 5.

**START STATE** The assembled structure suspended above the field.

**END STATE** Extreme wide. The whole world visible — plain, monolith field,
suspended structure. A single small warm light glows at the structure's centre.
The frame is composed with empty space that HTML type can sit in.

**GOOGLE FLOW PROMPT**

```
Cinematic extreme wide shot. The camera pulls back and rises steadily,
revealing the entire world at once: an immense foggy plain, a field of dark
monolithic stone structures, and one enormous assembled structure suspended
above them. At the centre of the suspended structure a single small warm amber
light begins to glow softly, the only warm colour in the frame. Everything else
remains bone and ash grey. Deep fog, overcast toplight, fine airborne dust. One
continuous slow pull-back, no cuts, ending on a still, balanced composition
with generous empty sky. Photorealistic, muted, film grain, desaturated,
monumental.
```

**CAMERA** One continuous pull-back and rise, decelerating to a near stop.

**SUBJECT MOTION** None but the light coming up.

**ENVIRONMENT** Fog and dust only.

**LIGHTING** A single warm amber point appears. No bloom explosion, no rays.

**CONTINUITY LOCK** Everything from Chapter 5, unchanged except the light.

**NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS** No text. No logos. No lens flare or god rays. No sun.
No additional lights. No cuts.

**DURATION** 8s · **ASPECT** 16:9 · **POST** None. This clip's last frame is the
site's transition point into HTML content, so it must end still.

## What happens when the clips arrive

1. QC each clip — contact sheet at 0/25/50/75/100%, checking identity, camera
   stability, and that each start state matches the previous end state.
2. Normalise to a common resolution and frame rate.
3. Concatenate into one master film; record chapter boundaries as scroll
   fractions.
4. Extract ~400 WebP frames at ~1400px wide.
5. Build the canvas scroll engine and the caption system on the chapter
   fractions.
6. Build the HTML site below the film, continuous with it.

Tooling is already verified: ffmpeg 6.1.1 with libwebp, and Pillow 12 as the
fallback encoder.
